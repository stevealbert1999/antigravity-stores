# LedgerGuard AI

Supervised AI for Accounts Payable teams.

LedgerGuard AI helps finance teams detect invoice duplicates, PO mismatches, aging issues, high-value anomalies, and weak approvals before payments are released.

## Public demo

https://stevealbert1999.github.io/antigravity-stores/accounts-payable-ai/demo/

## Positioning

LedgerGuard AI is not a payment bot. It is a risk review layer for Accounts Payable.

The system recommends, explains, and prioritizes. Finance approves.

## Target buyer

- CFO
- Controller
- AP Manager
- Finance Operations Manager
- Companies processing 300+ invoices/month
- Companies with ERP/AP data but manual review bottlenecks

## Initial pilot

30-day pilot focused on:

- duplicate invoice detection
- PO mismatch detection
- high-value invoice review
- aging prioritization
- audit-ready decision logs
- ROI report

## Commercial promise

Reduce manual AP review time, catch preventable payment errors, and give Finance a prioritized risk queue before approving payments.

## Safety boundary

LedgerGuard AI should not execute payments automatically in the initial product.

Recommended control model:

1. AI flags risk.
2. AI explains reason.
3. Human approves, rejects, or escalates.
4. Decision is logged.
