# LedgerGuard AI Discovery Call

## Goal
Understand AP workflow pain, invoice volume and approval process.

## Questions
1. How many invoices do you process monthly?
2. How are invoices reviewed today?
3. How often do duplicate invoices or PO mismatches appear?
4. What ERP/accounting workflow do you use?
5. How many people are involved in approvals?
6. What is the biggest bottleneck in AP today?
7. How important is auditability/compliance?

## Demo Flow
1. Upload CSV
2. Risk queue
3. Approval workflow
4. Audit log
5. ROI framing

Offer a 30-day pilot using anonymized historical AP exports.
