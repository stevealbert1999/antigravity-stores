# LedgerGuard AI Pilot Proposal

## Objective
Reduce AP review risk, improve invoice visibility and introduce explainable approval workflows.

## Pilot Scope
- Duration: 30 days
- Input source: anonymized CSV/AP export
- Invoice risk analysis
- Duplicate invoice detection
- PO mismatch review
- Approval workflow
- Audit log generation
- Weekly review session

## Deliverables
1. Risk dashboard access
2. AP risk reports
3. Approval/audit workflow
4. ROI summary
5. Production recommendations

## Client Requirements
- Historical AP export
- Finance stakeholder
- Weekly feedback session

## Pricing
- Pilot fee: 3,000 EUR
- Optional extended deployment: custom pricing

## Success Criteria
- Reduction in manual review time
- Detection of duplicate/high-risk invoices
- Positive AP workflow feedback
- ROI visibility within pilot window

## Important Note
LedgerGuard AI does not execute payments or replace ERP systems. The system assists prioritization, explanation and auditability.
